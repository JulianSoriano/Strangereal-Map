    Rosters come from each game's aircraft page on the Ace Combat Wiki. Air Combat, Ace Combat 2 and Ace Combat 04 used short in-game names (F-15, EF-2000, TND-IDS, R-M01, SF-39); these are matched to the nearest real variant. SF-35 and SF-39 are read as the Saab Draken and Gripen. Top Gun: Maverick versions of the F-14A and F/A-18E in Ace Combat 7 are folded into those rows.

    Ace Combat 3's corporate-era jets are placed under the real aircraft they descend from. Neucom, Ouroboros and Northern Wings designs have no real maker, so they sit with the other Strangereal originals; Northern Wings notes say which real jet each resembles.

    Dates through 1999 follow the Ace Combat Wiki's Strangereal timeline (a condensed reading; minor enlistments and transfers are left out). The wiki page I could reach stops partway through 1999, so entries from 2000 on come from the war briefings in this log rather than the timeline page.
